Scene 1:
Man Sitting by the tree speaking in a mix of language A and O, He's remembering something.

Scene 2:
Boy is running around the tree, shouting in language A.

State 2: 
Tree is lively, color/lighting is bright and vibrant

Boy and woman are sitting by the tree, talking in language A

State 3:
Tree is lively, color/lighting is bright and vibrant.

Plane is flying the background

State 4:
Tree is lively, color/lighting is bright and vibrant.

Boy and woman are sitting by the tree, talking in language A

Boy is by the tree on his own. He is translating words from language A to language O
